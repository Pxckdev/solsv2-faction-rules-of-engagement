SOLS - FACTION ROE // RULES

By

Pxck & Jah

Welcome to Stories Of Los Santos Beta Faction Roleplay. This is a serious, cinematic, text and voice based roleplay server built around realism and authentic Los Santos style RP. Our goal is a high-quality environment where factions, individuals and gangs develop meaningful stories and rivalries through structured, immersive interactions.

By joining or taking part in faction RP, you accept and agree to the rules below. Breaking them will result in disciplinary action. No exceptions.

FACTION ATTACKS

  • Max attackers: 4 players per coordinated attack. (Excluded if trouping/trouped.)
  • Restart buffer: no faction attacks or robberies within 15 minutes before or after a server restart.

CONFLICT BUILDUP & ESCALATION REQUIREMENTS

  • All required interactions must be developed over multiple days.
  • Minimum of eight (8) qualifying negative interactions before any shootout.
  • Verbal arguments do count.

ESCALATION DUE TO DIRECT DISRESPECT

  • Direct insults toward deceased associates may justify escalation only when supported by meaningful roleplay.

PROBATIONARY FACTIONS

  • Cannot attack, rob or commit hostile acts for 2 full days.
  • RP buildup and character development only.
  • Can defend themselves if attacked.

ESCALATION STANDARDS

  • Every attack must develop naturally through RP.
  • Warning shots are not attacks.
  • You can only be shot back at if you aim your gun at someone.

TURF & DEATHMATCHING

  • 2+ members on turf may be attacked.
  • Max 8 intentional kills per attack.
  • Proper identification required.
  • TOG tags may be used as pinpointing if masked or marked.

PERSONAL BEEF / INDIVIDUAL INTERACTIONS

  • Personal beef is allowed when properly documented.
  • Must remain between the involved individuals unless officially escalated.
  • No rushed violence.

MANDATORY /ME & /DO USAGE

  • Required for robberies, physical altercations, jump-outs and escalation.
  • Actions must be reactable and realistic.
  • If caught using subpar /me, /do or /ame, your faction will receive punishment. Examples: "/me would search the male" with no follow-up /do, or misspelling words in your /me, /do or /ame.

GENERAL FACTION REQUIREMENTS

  • All attackers must appear in the faction menu.
  • Non-faction members may not join attacks.
  • Reloading mid-attack is forbidden.

VEHICLE RULES

  • No exotic, donation or restricted vehicles.
  • Electric vehicles prohibited.
  • Motorcycles and dirt bikes prohibited.

ROBBERY RULES

  • Robbery cooldown: 1 hour.
  • Same faction robbery cooldown: 2 hours.
  • Five prior interactions required unless an obvious RP reason exists.
  • Robbing on turf or off turf counts as a faction attack.

BACKDOOR & ESCALATION RULES

  • Probation factions cannot backdoor.
  • Six interactions required in order to tie flags with another faction.
  • Must be properly built through RP.

FEAR RP & PLAYING TO WIN

  • Failure to fear RP is punishable.
  • Hiding weapons through emotes is prohibited.

MUTUAL COMBAT

  • 2+ members per faction engaging counts as faction conflict.
  • Max brawl size: 30 members per faction.

PUBLIC EVENTS

  • No shootings, beef or drama during advertised public events unless approved by faction management.

EVIDENCE & METAGAMING

  • Crosshair use results in faction termination.
  • Stream sniping and metagaming result in bans.
  • POVs must be provided upon request.

ADDITIONAL RULES

  • Remain on the server 15 minutes after attacks.
  • Your character must be 72 hours old before faction fights.
  • Water evading is prohibited.
  • Cheating results in wipes.

FACTION REPORTS

  • Must be filed within 24 hours.
  • Reports must be submitted by leaders.

POLICE ENGAGEMENTS

  • No combat logging or leaving active PD scenes.
  • Shooting PD requires valid escalation and life-threatening circumstances.

ADDITIONAL FIREARM RULES

  • Logged guns may be re-spun after 3 days.
  • Unregistered firearms may result in punishment.

FACTION MANAGEMENT RULES

  • If your faction has a player who is part of the faction management team, they can't take any tickets or handle anything involving your faction or whatever faction they're in.

FINAL REMINDER

This is a serious Los Santos based RP community. All interactions should prioritize immersion, respect, realism and believable character growth. Faction roleplay in SOLS Beta is expected to be realistic, well-developed and fair.
